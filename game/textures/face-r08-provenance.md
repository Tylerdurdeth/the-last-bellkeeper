# Atlas R08 facial colour map

Generated 24 September 2026 in the user's Atlas project "The Bellkeeper", Text → Image (Fast), Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite Image, 1:1 1K (about 4 credits). Replaces face-r07 after the owner found the old face unlikable.

`face-r08.webp` is the downloaded 1024×1024 PNG (source kept privately in references-v2/face/a7cf6871.png), warped with a piecewise-linear vertical remap and 0.86× horizontal feature spacing so brows, eyes, nose and mouth land on the rows code-character.js maps onto the procedural head, then WebP-encoded (q88). Eyes are still rendered procedurally (larger, rounder iris surfaces in hero-study-a.js); the texture supplies skin, brows, nose and mouth colour only. No image-to-3D or downloaded mesh.
