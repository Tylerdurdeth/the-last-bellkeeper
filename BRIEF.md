# The Last Bellkeeper — production contract

Current authorization: first representative playable scene only. Expansion requires review of this milestone and the user's decision; do not treat the scene as the complete game.

## Full adventure

An original 12–15-minute fantasy adventure with a fixed elevated, gently perspective 3D camera and painterly cel shading. An apprentice in a canopy village learns to capture and release wind with a copper bell staff. Mara believes the windworks guardian has seized; discovery reveals it stopped the outward current to protect a tree whose forgotten return channel is blocked. Restore the paired channels, calm the guardian, return to the changed village and complete the morning bell phrase. One distant bell answers.

One connected map: canopy village, root path, windworks. One mentor, one maintenance construct, one guardian, two puzzle sequences, one optional memory. No free camera rotation, precision jumping, climbing or additional combat system. Teach movement, interaction, capture, carry, assisted release and safe repositioning before testing them. Full title, skippable live introduction, settings, saves, checkpoints, touch access and ending remain mandatory.

## First scene: Borrow a breath

One rich canopy terrace and adjoining maintenance garden. The coral apprentice walks among ivory walkways, copper channels, a living tree, layered foliage, seed lanterns and a distant windworks silhouette. A repeatable bypass gust can be captured, carried and released into a stalled seed wheel. The wheel opens a service gate; seed pods and lanterns respond visibly. A short, clearly marked maintenance-vane hazard supplies a safe positioning/readability test using the same controls. Reset restores the starting state without reloading.

This is an expressly labeled vertical-slice test, not the story's final opening. Staff can be available for this focused test; the final adventure must retain gameplay acquisition after the intro. No title cinematic or entire route is claimed by this milestone.
