## Limits

The buttress tips remain broad, angular and somewhat cut off; these images do not establish fully organic sculpting or the original illustrative quality bar. The continuous joint is much better than base, but material contact is still a stylized edge rather than layered soil/moss integration. This is not a reason to reject the bounded improvement.

Only the provided chime endpoints were assessed. Small camera/pose differences prevent exact pixel comparison. Static screenshots cannot verify collisions, foot traversal over roots, changes on other slopes, camera-fade transitions, motion or performance. The reported geometry count and unchanged colliders were not independently tested here. This acceptance applies to visible joint/contact and endpoint readability only.
