# Rejected connected planting candidate

One bounded candidate replaced isolated shrub clumps along the cottage-to-garden route with lower overlapping fern banks and flattened root-contact fronds. All legacy random calls, rocks and collision geometry were preserved. Actual matched roots/west-path captures showed a regular roadside hedge effect; the lower silhouette was readable but less natural than the existing asymmetric clusters. Both author and lead rejected it, restoring the pre-candidate world code before runtime commit d7eb5a0. No Atlas credits spent. The unresolved issue is vegetation variety and integration with ground; this failed candidate is not a claimed quality gain. Selected before/after roots images are versioned; full source/captures remain locally at /tmp/bellkeeper-planting.
