## Two largest remaining repair hypotheses

### 1. Compose the completion reveal and conceal terminal terrain geometry

**Major — ART-03, rebuild restore/reward target. Single most damaging remaining visual gap.** Own desktop `runtime-overlook.png` is dominated by an enormous near tree/crown on the right and a dark rectangular terrain drop in the middle. Distant rocks are peripheral. Phone `phone/runtime-overlook.png` mostly shows hero, a rock, tree trunk and sheer green wall: the promised overlook provides no broad landscape reward. In both garden/chime views, a straight terrain boundary meets flat haze near the top, exposing the finite map. More geometry technically existing beyond the finish does not establish a vista.

**Repair hypothesis:** author a constrained finish framing that reveals the intended valley through a deliberate opening. Move/trim only the obstructing foreground crown and position several reused distant silhouettes at different depths; irregularly mask the visible straight terrain boundary and sheer rectangular edge with rock/bank/foliage structure. Preserve hero/control readability and collision safety. Do not expand playable geography.

**Acceptance:** actual approach → finish screenshots and continuous brief camera transition show foreground framing, readable hero/overlook ledge, middle valley and distant silhouette on desktop and430px portrait. No straight map edge, rectangular trench or giant close crown dominates the reward. Compare matched pre/post frames, not just a scene graph or far-away camera shot.

### 2. Preserve the body/hand/action silhouette within the denser plants and chime

**Major — TEST-02/04, ART-03.** Own chime frames show the copper cap across the apprentice's head/torso and hide the staff/hand action. This occurs on both desktop and phone. Dense shrubs also cover feet/lower body near the overlook; the prior motion retake documented additional body coverage during lateral travel. Density now competes with gameplay instead of merely supporting it.

**Repair hypothesis:** create a small open approach/interaction pocket at the chime so the player faces it with hands visible, or fade the cap locally when it blocks the hero. Trim/reposition bank edges that intersect the traversable corridor and destination standing point. Keep thick planting outside that space. Do not globally remove foliage or enlarge the whole scene.

**Acceptance:** normal-input approaches from at least two sides, chime interaction, capture and finish show head, staff grip and planted feet continuously at both viewports. The improved hand animation should be plainly assessable at game scale. Retain the dense banks in surrounding frame and preserve source discovery rather than adding destination arrows.
