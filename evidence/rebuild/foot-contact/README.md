# Grounded stride contact diagnostic

An independent physical constraint exposed a real animator mismatch: the avatar moved farther over a stance interval than its foot moved backward relative to the body. Distance-driven cycle timing alone did not prevent skating. On a stationary floor with actual hero joints and animation code, steady walking had roughly0.83m/s forward ankle drift and running roughly0.65m/s. The new reach derives from the distance travelled during stance, preserving controller speed and cadence. Grounded thighs counter the pelvis yaw so expressive hip motion does not drag ankles sideways.

`tools/test-foot-contact.mjs` observes rendered foot-group bounds and world ankle displacement at30/60/120Hz, after a one-second settle. It does not copy the gait formula. The unmodified previous module fails the new contact test (before.json); the current candidate passes (after.json). Median planar drift is approximately0.035m/s walking and0.085–0.093m/s running; stance soles remain within roughly2cm of the floor. The0.1m/s median limit permits about a centimetre of ankle movement over a running stance, not a perfect frozen contact point. The90th-percentile limit is0.2m/s, allowing brief foot-roll/stance-boundary motion. These are synthetic flat-floor metrics, not human polish approval, terrain IK, or proof of every turn/landing. Actual normal-input visual comparison is required before retaining the change.
