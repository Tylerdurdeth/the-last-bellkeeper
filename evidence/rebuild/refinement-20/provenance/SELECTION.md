# Actual geometry selection

The adjacent `references` folder contains the two Atlas outputs. `instruments/{a,b,c}` and `life/{a,b,c}` contain authentic recipe reports and five-view contact sheets. All 30 candidates passed. These images show rendered geometry, not concept art. Selected modules are in `game/assets/`.

| Asset | Candidate | Selection reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gong | B | Curved branch uprights and a dished bronze face, not a straight-post sign. |
| Seed bells | B | Open ceramic mouths on an organic root frame, not eggs or cones. |
| Wind harp | B | Shaped foot, copper fittings, strings and teal pennant. The final version adds the missing pennant mast; reverified separately. |
| Sanctuary bell | B | Flared bell, patina rings, rooted stone piers and a sun crown distinguish the destination. |
| Bramble | B | Dense leaf mound and intertwined thorn canes, less blocky than A. |
| Hosta | C | Broad curved and ribbed blades, rather than A's oval pads or B's graphic wedges. |
| Shelf stump | B | Hollow broken crown, root flares and convex amber fungi. |
| Rabbit | A | Rounded haunches and thick ears remain readable from the side. |
| Kingfisher | A | Compact rounded body, long bill, folded wings and orange/cream markings. |
| Luna moth | B | Scalloped profiled wings, eye spots, veins and long hindwing tails. |

The life variants each remain below 4,000 triangles. A/B final life sheets include visual refinements; unchanged C retains its original verification. The final harp has 1,172 triangles. The existing root chime's retained timber bracket is also verified in `instruments/final`.

Integration preserves rabbit head/ear and moth wing pivots. Kingfishers perch on raycast stump geometry and do not fly with rigid wings. The bounded life factory adds 71,676 triangles across all its objects; some additional hostas replace previous repeated fern/mushroom scatter. Static vegetation is batched. This is a low-poly interpretation, not a claim that the painted references were reproduced exactly.
