## Repairs, ranked (three maximum)

1. **Major — staff grip and release direction (ANIM-02/TEST-04).** At frame 053, the extended tool-side hand is visibly below the diagonal staff shaft; the shaft passes above the hand rather than through a readable palm grip. The bell/ring is behind the head/shoulder while the bare shaft end points toward the wheel. This makes the signature action look like a floating prop rather than a controlled bell gesture. Run frames 011–018 also carry the shaft horizontally with the bell behind the upper body, so fixing only the single release pose is insufficient. **Repair:** correct the staff's local grip transform relative to the animated hand, then constrain wrist/forearm pose so bell end leads capture/release and remains clear of face. Preserve actual hand parenting but do not mistake parenting for visual contact. **Acceptance:** front, side and back-three-quarter normal-input walk/run/capture/release show continuous palm-to-shaft contact, bell clearly leading the gesture, no face/shoulder penetration. Include gameplay scale and diagnostic crop; inspect motion, not merely one frame.

2. **Major — running lacks a convincing whole-body effort silhouette (ANIM-01/TEST-04).** Frames 011–024 show alternating stride and knee lift, but the trunk/head remain almost vertically stacked through the run; the narrow straight torso and forward tool arm dominate. The leg changes alone do not communicate the energetic confident movement requested. This is a pose observation, not a claim that speed or stride telemetry failed. **Repair:** author a stronger coherent forward hip/chest lean and counter-rotation through shoulders, with a clearly bent pumping free arm; retain tool control rather than swinging the shaft through the face. Avoid exaggerated head bob. **Acceptance:** distinguish run from walk in silhouette in side and back-three-quarter gameplay captures, with purposeful acceleration/braking poses; normal-speed inspection must show feet support the forward mass without skating. Preserve responsive movement speed.

3. **Minor-to-major charisma gap — idle/action intent is too subdued (ANIM-01/TEST-02).** Idle frames 000–003 and settled frames 030–035 remain a nearly parallel-footed upright pose. R01's broad elbow separation, asymmetric weight and directed head/face do not read here. Capture frames 044–046 open the arms, but the back-facing view and small body motion give little visible anticipation/intent. **Repair:** establish one asymmetric grounded idle with a clear free-elbow angle and head orientation, then have head/shoulder commit toward source/target before the arm beat. Keep gestures brief and the player responsive; do not slow the whole game. **Acceptance:** gameplay-scale front/three-quarter idle and capture have a recognizable intentional silhouette distinct from neutral locomotion. Record the entire anticipation→contact→follow-through sequence at normal speed; feet remain planted during stationary gestures.
