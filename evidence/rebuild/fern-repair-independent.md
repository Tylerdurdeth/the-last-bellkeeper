# Independent fern repair selection

**Select B for the bounded fern-form repair.** Directly inspected base/A/B west-path views, base/B chimes, B roots, B portrait west-path/chimes, and the original `woodland-r02.png` reference. No browser run, implementation, asset change or spending by this reviewer.

B reads recognizably as fern fronds: leaflets follow connected arching stems with taper and a coherent overall silhouette. Base reads more like rounded clumps of separate polygonal leaves. A fixes the connection but is visibly wiry and sparse; B's broader curved leaflets restore enough visual weight to read at the gameplay camera. This is closer to the reference's lower-corner fern vocabulary without claiming its illustration quality.

There is a real density tradeoff: B occupies less opaque foliage area than base, particularly around the chime clearing. It does not preserve identical fullness. I nevertheless prefer B: the rock-and-fern west bank remains layered, the individual plants look intentional, and the painted ground now carries the spaces between them. The hero, cream route, flower border and chime stay clear on desktop and portrait. No new consequential endpoint readability defect is visible in the inspected images.

The reference remains substantially richer in overlapping vegetation, irregular plant grouping and finely shaped fronds. B still has repeated radial arrangements and relatively simple leaflet shapes; this is not benchmark parity or approval of overall scene richness. Do not compensate automatically by scattering more plants into interaction spaces.

Static supplied screenshots support form, local density and endpoint readability only. They do not verify wind motion, shimmer/aliasing in motion, performance, collisions, full-route occlusion or a physical phone. Capture positions differ slightly; the comparison is compositional rather than pixel-registered. Reported recipe and runtime checks are separate producer evidence, not independently rerun here.
