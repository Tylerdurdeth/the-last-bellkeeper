Status: isolated proposal only; not approved or merged. Author recommendation is reject, subject to lead/independent review. No production files edited. No Atlas, API, purchase, reset or other paid-tool calls. Lead meters shared existing Codex credit allowance.

## Concrete composition attempted

Used existing recipe-verified repaired fern and flower prototypes around existing tracked/collidable rocks near(-19,.2),(-18.2,.9),(-18.3,-3), beneath the west-bend tree. Removed legacy visual plants only inside one2.8×3m-radius elliptical pocket, after all original random scale/rotation arguments were evaluated. Designed one tall rear cluster, low front fan and an open notch between rocks, rather than a continuous roadside border. No new geometry or assets; no new rocks. Existing rocks, their tracked surfaces, trunk collision, path, shortcut, garden clearances and random-call sequence retained.

The original woodland-r02 illustration uses root/stone/plant overlap with gaps revealing soil and depth. This candidate tested that structure at a single photographed bend; it does not recreate its atmosphere or add landscape relief.

Reject recommendation: the newly authored taller rear plants are mostly hidden behind the near trunk/root in the gameplay camera. Clearing old front plants exposes more of the rock, but opens an empty strip without replacing it with a strong layered silhouette. Portrait loses useful foreground foliage and is visibly sparser. The open notch is readable, but that alone does not meet the requested richer natural environment. The candidate is different, not demonstrably better. Existing baseline should remain.

This result is not an approval of baseline planting or the overall reference-art bar. It identifies a concrete placement failure: world-space rear layering was not sufficiently visible in the elevated camera. No extra variants or expansion were made. No performance claim from concurrent headless runs; no new colliders or changes to gameplay to test.
