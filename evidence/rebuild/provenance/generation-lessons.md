# Lessons from the pilot and rebuild

The failed2-credit orchestration attempt demonstrated a workflow/budget-envelope failure, not an artistic verdict. Do not repeat the same opaque paid dispatch. The subsequent direct image nodes produced inspectable downloaded outputs at5credits each; reserve the configured maximum before dispatch and reconcile the actual settlement after completion.

A useful image prompt has one role and a clear output contract. Separate the canonical character portrait, woodland composition and seamless surface textures. An environment image's incidental person is not permission to change the established hero. Avoid style boards with illegible tiny objects or generated labels. For materials, specify broad painted strokes, subdued contrast, seamless tiling, no cast shadows and no complete scene; strong microtexture competes with silhouettes at phone size.

On the observed Atlas UI, a programmatic field value was not enough to persist an edit. Actual typing, blur and rereading the displayed prompt/settings were necessary before Run became a meaningful new dispatch. Record the exact persisted prompt rather than a longer draft that never ran. A stale cached image is not a new successful generation.

Credits buy references and image/audio outputs, not automatically coherent Three.js composition or motion. The largest subsequent improvements came from geometry, camera clearance, physical story props, correctly compiled transparency and actual-input reviews. More generations would not fix those defects. Material alternatives were compared in the real scene and rejected when they made trunks flatter or darker.

The paid2-second SFX node completed but reliable permitted file retrieval was not established. Its9credits remain in the ledger and its output is not shipped. No additional sound generation was purchased to work around that retrieval problem. Original procedural audio is a working fallback; technical graph/sample validation does not establish listening quality.
