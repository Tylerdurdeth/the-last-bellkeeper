Nine local visual passes produced a new skinned Bellkeeper in the minimal character studio. Default now selects the candidate; authored mannequin and original procedural baseline remain selectable. The woodland game is unchanged.

Changes: CC0 continuous head/body, enlarged teal eye regions, relaxed brows, narrowed jaw and nose, shortened chestnut hair, coral/teal outfit, cream scarf and turquoise fastening, copper-coloured bell accessory. Existing authored locomotion transfers through bind-space rotations and retains target bone lengths. Face uses a softer lighting ramp; clothing retains toon bands. Source licenses and provenance ship alongside assets.

Rejected during review: stern stock anatomy, oversized scarf bib, globally teal eye whites, high-contrast baked facial shadows. Final selected screenshots: candidate-09/face.png, three.png, run-2.png. Full intermediate evidence remains local.

Validation: desktop and emulated phone load; all three sources; idle/walk/jog/sprint/jump/turn selection; freeze/frame-step; keyboard movement; real CDP touch events. No browser errors. Actual iPhone performance has not been verified.

Assessment: materially different from the primitive baseline, with accepted authored motion intact. Still below the supplied Zelda character references. Jaw/mouth anatomy, hair silhouette and eyelid/expression animation remain weak; no facial rig/blinking yet. This is a playable design checkpoint, not aesthetic acceptance. Further small shader tweaks cannot substitute for a better authored facial sculpt and expression rig.
