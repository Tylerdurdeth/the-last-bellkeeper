# Face repair

Rebuilt the default study face after user rejection. A smooth interpolated cheek/jaw profile replaces the stretched sphere; integrated nose projection is reduced, left/right eye contours mirrored, irises brightened, pupils reduced, brows tapered, lips constructed as curved surface strips. Subtle procedural skin colour and softer facial lighting replace the broad angular facial bands. Neck narrows into the head. No downloaded geometry or image changes. No Atlas spending.

Four local passes inspected front, profile, rear and locomotion; first scalloped profile and overly dark nasal shading rejected. Final remains a simplified stylized face, below the expressiveness of Atlas R06. Prior B/C studies retained for comparison. Changes are isolated to the studio.

Local studio regression passes desktop/emulated touch, all sources, all clips, movement, freeze/frame step and zero mesh requests/browser errors. Selected capture: face-repair-4/face.png.
